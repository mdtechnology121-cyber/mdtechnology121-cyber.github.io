Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 26, 2026

Data Collection

MD Technology does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app communicates only with Instagram's public GraphQL API to fetch video URLs you explicitly request, and with CDN servers to download the video file.

What We Access

When you paste an Instagram URL and tap Download, the app sends that URL's shortcode to Instagram's API. No login credentials, cookies, or personal identifiers are sent. The video is saved locally to Movies/VideoDownloader.

No Account, No Tracking

No accounts, no analytics SDKs, no ads, no tracking. No data leaves your device except the single API request.

Your Rights (GDPR/CCPA)

Since we collect no data, there is nothing to access, delete, or port. Contact us with any concerns.
