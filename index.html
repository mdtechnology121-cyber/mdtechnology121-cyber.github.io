Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

Is this app safe?
Yes. MD TECHNOLOGY is open-source, contains no malware, no trackers, and requests no unnecessary permissions. It only accesses the network to fetch video URLs and download files you explicitly request.
Do I need an Instagram account?
No. The app works without any Instagram login. You only need the public URL of the reel or video you want to download.
Where are downloaded videos saved?
Videos are saved to Movies/VideoDownloader on your device's internal storage for easy access.
Can I download private Instagram content?
No. The app can only access publicly available Instagram content, just like visiting a public page in a browser.
Is this legal?
You are responsible for complying with Instagram's Terms of Service and applicable copyright laws. Only download content you have the right to access.
Why is the APK not on Google Play?
Google Play restricts apps that download video content from other platforms. The APK is available here for direct download and sideloading.
